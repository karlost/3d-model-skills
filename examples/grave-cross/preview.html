Grave cross in lava

A graveyard cross stuck into a lump of fake lava, slightly crooked, with small bones half sunk round its foot. The LED string already on the lava lights the white cross from below. All white PLA. Sizes are in mm and use the same names as the sketch and generate.py. Drag to rotate.

grave-cross.3mf has two plates and one filament, so nothing changes during the print. 0.20 mm Standard, every part passed printcheck "OK without supports".

With the spike the cross is mm long and only just fits the 256 mm P1S plate.

Build steps

  1. Print both plates.
  2. Colour the engraved with a black marker, otherwise white on white does not show.
  3. Make a hole in the middle of the lava with a screwdriver, push the cross in slightly crooked and fill round the foot with hot glue.
  4. Lay the bones round the foot of the cross and hot-glue them.
  5. Run the LED string round the foot so the LEDs light the cross from below.

Why it is like this

  • Flat cross lying on its back: prints without supports and only mm high, so in under an hour. The bed face is flat, the top-face edges are chamfered at 45°.
  • Chamfer in 0.2 mm steps: matches the layer height, the printer makes it that way anyway.
  • Letters mm, engraved mm: the strokes are wider than 1 mm, so they print.
  • Bones as halves: the flat bottom is the sunk half and the face on the bed.

Change it by name

Each size is one line at the top of generate.py. Ask for a change by name, e.g. "SPAN 110" or "TEXT BOO", then run it again.
